Free phone charging station for your bar

Charged YYJ installs a phone charging station in your bar at no cost, pays you 15% of every $9 rental (before tax), and handles all the upkeep. Your staff never handle payments or chargers. Just keep drinks off it.

  • Cost to your venue$0

  • PayoutsMonthly
  • Staff workHands-off
  • Power neededOne regular 120 V outlet
  • CommitmentMonth to month, 14 days' notice

What you get

A countertop station with eight power bank slots, a 10.1-inch screen and a card reader. Guests tap their credit card on the station, pay $9 and take a power bank with them, so they don't need a working phone to rent. Your staff never handle payments, cables or phones.

It's a small thing guests remember: they can stay longer and still have enough battery to get home. And you stop lending out the cable behind the bar.

How you get paid

Your venue earns 15% of every $9 rental made at your station, calculated before tax. We pay it out monthly by e-transfer, with a rental report. A rental that's refunded in full, or that ends in the $39 charge for a power bank that isn't returned, earns no share. There are no fees, no equipment cost and no minimum number of rentals.

What we take care of

  • Install: we bring the station, set it up on your counter and plug it into one regular 120 V outlet. No wiring or drilling.
  • Service: we keep the station running and look after any issues.
  • Swaps: we rebalance and swap power banks so your station keeps stock for guests.
  • Support: guests with questions contact Charged YYJ, not your staff.

No long contract

The arrangement is month to month. If it isn't working for you, give us 14 days' notice and we'll come and pick the station up. No penalties.

Where we're starting

Charged YYJ is launching December 2026, with stations in bars and venues around downtown Victoria. Power banks can be returned to any Charged YYJ station, so every venue that joins makes the service more useful for guests across the city.

We're a local Victoria business, and we're looking for venues now. If you'd like one for your bar, pub, lounge or venue, reach out and we'll come by to look at where it would fit.
