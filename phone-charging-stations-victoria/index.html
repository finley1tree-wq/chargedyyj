Phone charging stations in Victoria, BC bars

Charged YYJ is bringing self-serve phone charging stations to bars and venues in downtown Victoria, BC. Each one holds charged power banks that guests can rent with one tap of a credit card and take with them. They charge phones, not electric cars.

What the station looks like

The station is a compact countertop unit built for a busy bar. It has eight slots for power banks, a 10.1-inch screen on the front and a card reader. The screen shows guests how to rent and they tap their card to pay, so there's nothing for staff to explain.

  • Power bank slots8
  • Screen10.1-inch
  • PaymentTap-to-pay card reader
  • PlacementCountertop
  • PowerOne regular 120 V outlet
  • Rental price$9 flat, PST included

It plugs into one regular 120 V wall outlet. There's no wiring, drilling or special electrical work. The power banks charge in the station while they wait for the next guest.

How guests use it

A guest taps a Visa, Mastercard or American Express credit card on the station's card reader, pays $9 and takes the power bank the station releases. The card is all they need, so a dead phone is no problem. Each power bank is 5,000 mAh and has Lightning, USB-C and Micro-USB cables built in, so it works with iPhones and Android phones without anyone needing to bring a cord.

Guests return the power bank to any Charged YYJ station within 24 hours. The price, card hold and non-return charge are on the power bank rental page.

Where the stations will be

We're launching December 2026, with stations in bars and venues around downtown Victoria. Because you can return a power bank to any station, the more venues that join, the easier it is for guests to rent at one spot and drop it off at another later in the night.

We'll post station locations on this site as they're installed.

Why bars are a good fit

Phones die at night. People use them all evening for photos, maps, messages, splitting tabs and getting a ride home. A charging station on the counter lets guests top up without staff lending out their own chargers or keeping guests' phones behind the bar.

For venues, the station is free and earns a share of every rental. You can read how that works on our page for bars and venues.
